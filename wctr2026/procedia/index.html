How the process runs

  1. Authors send the final manuscript and the response to reviewers. We are here
  2. The editorial team checks each paper against the template and the 10-page limit, and writes to authors only if something needs correcting.
  3. The complete issue is transferred to Elsevier in one package.
  4. Elsevier typesets the papers and contacts corresponding authors directly for proofs.
  5. The issue is published online in Transportation Research Procedia.

Elsevier has not yet confirmed a publication date. It will be posted here as soon as it is known.

Common questions

I sent my files but the page says you are waiting for them.

This page is updated in batches, so recent emails may not be counted yet. If your files were sent before the date at the top of the page, please resend them in reply to our email, with the paper number in the subject line.

My paper is longer than 10 pages. Can it still be published?

Only in a version of 10 pages or fewer in the Procedia template. Elsevier does not accept longer papers for this issue. Please send the shortened version by the deadline.

Do I need a new response to reviewers if I only shorten the paper?

No. A new response is needed only if the content changes or if we told you that we do not have one on file.

My paper number is not found.

The page lists only papers that are in the Procedia process. Papers graded A or B and invited to a journal special issue are on the special issues status page. If you believe your paper should be here, write to the address above with your paper number.

I have decided to publish elsewhere.

Please send a one-line email saying so, with your paper number, so that we can close your file.

When will I receive proofs, a DOI or an acceptance letter?

Proofs and the DOI come from Elsevier after the issue has been transferred. The editorial team cannot issue them earlier.
