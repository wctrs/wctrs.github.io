How the special issue route works

  1. The chair of your Special Interest Group arranges a special issue with a journal and acts as its lead guest editor.
  2. Once the journal has agreed, the SIG chair invites the authors and sends the submission instructions and deadline.
  3. You revise your paper, address the WCTR reviewers' comments in a response letter, and submit through the journal's own system.
  4. The paper goes through the journal's normal peer review. Publication is decided by that review and is not guaranteed.

Common questions

I have not heard from my SIG chair.

Check the table above. If the special issue for your SIG is still in discussion, invitations have not gone out yet. If it is agreed and you have not received an invitation, write to your SIG chair with your paper number. Their addresses are in the table above.

Can I publish in Transportation Research Procedia instead?

Yes. Publication in the conference proceedings is guaranteed if the reviewers' comments are addressed, but the paper must be at most 10 pages. See the Procedia status page for what to send and the deadline.

My SIG has no special issue.

Not every SIG has arranged one. You may publish in Procedia, or submit your paper to a journal of your choice independently.

Where do I see whether my submitted paper is accepted?

In the journal's own submission system. This page shows only which special issue your paper is invited to; it does not track the journal's review.
